Career Habits and Goal Setting

Before you start

WOOP is different from other exercises: it involves thoughts and images rather than rational or effortful thinking. It involves going slow, creating time and space for thinking and imagining.

It is critical that no interruptions occur during the exercise. Start when you feel calm and comfortable. This is your time now. Everything else has to wait. Clear your mind and create space to imagine.

After going through today's lesson, think about your career aspirations and the steps you would like to take to achieve them.
